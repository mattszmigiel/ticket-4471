  • parser: the shared parser reads every date month first, so day-first tenants get a flipped month and day. This is the cause #4471 names.
  • hint: booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under the date field. Day-first tenants (alder.physio, copper.studio, pale.harbour) type what the hint says and the form reads it their way. Different bug, different fix.
  • shift: the date is off by one day, with day and month intact. A timezone or DST problem is the suspect. Not confirmed.

How the reports were counted

  • The export repeats some reports. One id is one report (uniqueById).
  • Intake's tag is a guess from the browser. It is wrong for many reports, so the count uses the tenant, the channel and the desk's reading of the report instead.
  • Five reports read as a one-day move (oneDayMove in desk-notes.json): R-41202, R-41209, R-41236, R-41344, R-41620. They count as shift.
  • Three parser reports carry no date to check (R-41290, R-41417, R-41514). They are placed by tenant only.
